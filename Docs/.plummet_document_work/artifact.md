## Page system

- A4 portrait throughout (8.27 × 11.69 in).
- Typical margins: left 1.18 in, right 0.98 in, top 0.94 in, bottom 0.86 in.
- Cover page uses a looser vertical composition and no visible page number.
- Preliminary pages use lower-case Roman numerals in the centered footer; the main body restarts at Arabic page 1.
- Page breaks are deliberate between front-matter components and major chapters.

## Typography and hierarchy

- Times New Roman is the governing typeface.
- Body copy is approximately 12 pt, justified, with generous 1.5-line leading.
- Front-matter titles are centered, bold, and uppercase.
- Chapter headings are bold uppercase at approximately 14 pt.
- Numbered subsection headings are bold at approximately 12 pt.
- Figure captions are centered and italic, using the `Figure x.y:` pattern.
- Academic prose remains predominantly black on white; decoration is restrained.

## Repeated components

- Cover: semester/project line, project title, university mark, author block, college, submission authority, month/year.
- Student declaration with registration table and signature lines.
- Recommendation and certificate pages with signatory blocks.
- Acknowledgement and abstract.
- Table of contents, list of figures, and abbreviations.
- Numbered chapters with nested headings, figures, tables, algorithms/flows, conclusion, and references.
